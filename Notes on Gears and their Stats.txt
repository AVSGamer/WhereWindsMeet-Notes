[When hunting for heirloom gears]
Once the gear is relayed, the affix stats' ceiling for value/amount
becomes the same for all affixes. So it doesn't matter what rarity
the affix was before, you can just raise it via modulating stones
upto a cap of 97% of a non-relayed gear's cap. Using relayed gears
is just about 2.7% weaker than a truly maxed out gear native to
that tier. The kind of stats combination itself matters more than
a all affixes max-value-tuned native gear with even just 1 wrong
stat affixed. This means we don't need to use Gold Tuners/Shells
everytime, specially when the gear is soon to be relayed.

[CRate & ARate Calculations]
Given we have 8 Agilt stats which would translate to around
32% CRate in this patch. To make that 32% CRate, which does not include
other sources of CRate as just for an example, translate into 80%.
25% of that 32% is what we need as our upper limit for our ARate or
lower. Why 25%? using algebra we can see 32% as 80% and the ? as 20%.
Get the factor; 20%/80% = 1/4th which is 0.25 or 25% in decimal or %.
The amount of ARate we should have should not exceed 8% if we are to
couple that with a 32% CRate and want to end up with 80%CRate and
20%ARate for our Precision Hits. But that is just from the Agilt
stats/affixes of our gears so we need to take into account the other
sources of CRate and base ARate too when fine-tuning for 100/80/20
Final Rates of a Critical Build or the other one; 100/60/40 for Affinity
Build. Count all Affinity Rate, Critical Rate, and Precision Rate from
your Basic Talents which you get for submitting Oddities as a 'Base'
Add to that your World Level Bonuses which right now is only 17.8%PRate.
The result of that is your 'Base Attack Type Rates' before Gears,
InnerWays and Rates against same-level or other players scaling comes in.

Another Example Scenario of the Final Rates Calculation;
12% CRate and 2% ARate
12+2=14
12/14=6/7 and 2/14=1/7
6/7=85.71% CR and 1/7=14.29% AR
Capping comes after above(scaling with the priority to preserve not yet
capped stat). Overflow percentages from capped stat gets transferred to
the other stat if you have 100%PRate since precision hits can only be
either Critical or Affinity.
12%CRate and 2%ARate gets translated into Final Rates: 80%CRate & 20%ARate
of a Precision Hit.

[Bow+Ring Set]
Bow Set Bonus is half of what an affix would give assuming same level/tiers;
Affinity/Critical/Precision. Best choice would be Precision.
You can change it whichever according to your stat adjustment needs though.
Precision is just more viable long term because of that 'Resistance'.
If you're building non-precision build, how are you even getting Abrasion
Rates? Abrasion is a ?gimped Normal Attack?. The Abrasion Rate is the rate
that when a Abrasion is about to happen, it gets converted back to a Normal
damage instead.

You have options whether you want to fine-tune min-max by taking into account
other sources of the rates.. Or just meeting the minimum with your other sources
like 'from Oddities and World Level' and gears only, makes it so that you can
play around switching other innerways because you no longer need the stats
from those, they would just be extras as you are really after the effects of
the skill instead of the stats.
Since InnerWays give the right stat bonuses mostly for their corresponding
Martial Path or intended use.

D.CRa and D.ARa is added on top of your capped/uncapped rates and unaffected
by resistances, so you can theoretically get
100% Critical Rate(20%D.CRa+80%CappedCRate) or
100% Affinity Rate(60%D.ARa+40%CappedARate).
